Our story

Every gift is arranged, not assembled.

Creative Gifting By Madniah Patel started as a small table of hand-tied bouquets and grew into a full gifting studio. Every cake, bouquet and hamper is planned around the person receiving it — their favourite colours, their celebration, their story — and put together by hand, not pulled off a shelf.

  • Custom colour palettes and themes for every order
  • Same-week turnaround for most occasions
  • Careful, presentation-first packaging
